Free Roam Bus

Drive anywhere in an endless city. No timer, no rules.

  • Hold Gas to go, Brake to stop, then hold it to reverse.
  • Follow the yellow arrow above your bus to the nearest stop. Slow down inside the amber ring to pick up passengers.
  • Tap Steer to switch between arrow buttons and a steering wheel. Drag the wheel left or right to turn it.
